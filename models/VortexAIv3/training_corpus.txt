Physics: Newton's second law is F = m a. Momentum is p = m v. Kinetic energy is E = 1/2 m v^2. Potential energy near Earth is E = m g h. Gravity accelerates objects near Earth at about 9.81 meters per second squared. Ohm's law is V = I R. Power is P = V I.

Math: addition combines numbers, subtraction finds differences, multiplication repeats addition, and division distributes a quantity. The quadratic formula is x = (-b plus or minus sqrt(b squared minus 4ac)) divided by 2a. The area of a circle is pi r squared. The circumference is 2 pi r. The Pythagorean theorem is a squared plus b squared equals c squared.

Programming: C++ uses classes, functions, variables, loops, and conditionals. Python emphasizes readable syntax and indentation. JavaScript runs in browsers and servers. GDScript is used by Godot. C# can be used with Godot. Luau is used by Roblox. Debugging means reproducing a problem, finding its cause, making a small fix, and testing again.

ESP32: the ESP32 is a microcontroller with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. The ESP32-2432S028 CYD commonly uses an ILI9341 320 by 240 display, SPI pins MOSI 13, MISO 12, SCLK 14, LCD CS 15, LCD DC 2, touch CS 33, and SD CS 5. GPIO 21 is commonly used for the display backlight. RAM is limited, so large AI models need quantization, streaming, or retrieval from SD storage.

Godot: Godot is a game engine. GDScript is its main scripting language and resembles Python. Nodes form scenes, scenes can be instanced, and signals connect events. Godot 4 uses CharacterBody3D for many player controllers and MeshInstance3D for 3D meshes.

Roblox: Roblox games use Luau scripts. ServerScriptService is for server scripts, ReplicatedStorage is shared storage, StarterGui contains player UI, and RemoteEvents can communicate between client and server with server validation.
