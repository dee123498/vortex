VORTEX PICO + CYD SD CARD

Format the card as FAT32.

Create:
/vortex/
  model.bin
  tokenizer.bin
  wifi.txt
  users.csv
  knowledge/

wifi.txt:
  line 1 = Wi-Fi SSID
  line 2 = Wi-Fi password

users.csv:
  guest,Guest
  user1,User 1
  user2,User 2
  user3,User 3

The CYD reads the SD card. The Pico does not directly access the CYD SD reader.
The Pico has its own 264 KB RAM limit, so do not assume a 128 GB card gives the Pico more working memory.
